# Privacy Policy – Savion

**Last Updated:** July 2026

Welcome to **Savion**.

Savion is an AI-powered personal knowledge inbox designed to help you capture, organize, and revisit important information through AI-powered insights, reminders, and daily summaries.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Savion processes, how it is used, and how we protect your data.

---

# Information We Process

Savion processes only the information that you intentionally choose to provide or create within the app. This may include:

* Shared text and web links
* Clipboard content that you explicitly choose to save
* Images and screenshots shared with the app
* Photos selected using the Android Photo Picker
* Documents and files selected using the system document picker
* Photos captured using the in-app camera
* Audio recordings created using the in-app recording feature
* Reminder dates and timestamps detected from your content
* App settings and preferences

Savion does **not** continuously monitor your device or collect information without your knowledge.

---

# How Information Is Used

The information you choose to provide is used only to deliver the features you request, including:

* Generating AI-powered insights and summaries
* Detecting reminders, appointments, and important dates
* Organizing your saved information
* Delivering reminder notifications and helpful nudges
* Improving your productivity experience within the app

Content is processed only for the functionality that you explicitly initiate.

---

# Local Storage

Most of your saved content is stored locally on your device.

Savion does **not** continuously monitor your personal activity or access your files in the background.

Files, photos, and other content are accessed only when you explicitly choose to share, import, capture, or record them using the app.

---

# AI Processing

When you choose to use AI-powered features, selected content may be securely transmitted to Google's AI services to generate:

* AI summaries
* Insights
* Reminder suggestions
* Content organization
* Other AI-powered responses

For files such as images, documents, or audio recordings, Savion may securely upload the selected file to Google's AI File service as part of the AI processing workflow. The uploaded file is used only to perform the AI task you requested.

Only the content necessary to provide the requested AI functionality is transmitted.

Savion does not upload or process your content for AI analysis unless you explicitly initiate an AI feature.

---

# Permissions

Savion requests permissions only when they are required for features that you choose to use. Sensitive permissions are requested at runtime and can be denied without affecting the core functionality of the app.

| Permission                    | Purpose                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Internet**                  | Securely communicate with Google AI services to generate AI-powered insights and summaries.                    |
| **Network State**             | Detect network availability before performing AI operations.                                                   |
| **Notifications**             | Deliver reminders, daily insights, and helpful notifications.                                                  |
| **Camera**                    | Capture photos or documents directly within the app for AI analysis when you choose to use the camera feature. |
| **Microphone (Record Audio)** | Record voice notes or audio for transcription and AI-powered insights when you explicitly start recording.     |

**Selecting Photos and Files**

Savion uses Android's built-in system pickers to let you choose content:

* **Android Photo Picker** for selecting photos and images.
* **System Document Picker** for selecting documents, PDFs, text files, and other supported file types.

These system pickers allow you to choose exactly which files to share with Savion. The app does not request broad access to your device storage.

---

# Permissions We Do Not Request

Savion does **not** request access to:

* SMS messages
* Contacts
* Call logs
* Phone state
* Background location
* Background camera access
* Background microphone access
* Broad storage access

---

# Third-Party Services

Savion may use trusted third-party services to provide app functionality, including:

* Google AI services
* Google AI SDK
* Google AI File service
* Firebase services (if enabled in future versions)

These services may process only the information required to provide the functionality you request and are governed by their own privacy policies.

---

# Data Security

Protecting your information is important to us.

Savion follows industry-standard security practices, including:

* Encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS) when communicating with external AI services.
* Runtime permission requests before accessing sensitive device features.
* Accessing files, photos, camera, and microphone only after your explicit action.
* Limiting transmitted information to only what is necessary for the requested AI feature.
* Local storage for most user content whenever possible.

Savion does not continuously monitor your device, camera, microphone, or files.

---

# Your Control

You remain in control of your information at all times.

You can:

* Grant or revoke app permissions through your device settings.
* Choose which files, photos, documents, or audio recordings to share with Savion.
* Delete your saved content from within the app.
* Clear app data or uninstall the application at any time to remove locally stored information.

---

# Children's Privacy

Savion is not designed for or directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.
