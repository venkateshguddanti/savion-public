# Privacy Policy – Savion

*Last Updated: May 2026*

Savion is designed to help users capture, organize, and revisit important information through AI-powered insights, reminders, and daily summaries.

## Information We Process

Savion may process content that users intentionally save or share into the app, including:

* Shared text and links
* Clipboard content saved by the user
* Images and screenshots shared into the app
* Reminder timestamps detected from user content
* App settings and preferences

## How Information Is Used

Savion uses this information to:

* Generate AI-powered insights and summaries
* Detect reminders and appointments
* Deliver notifications and nudges
* Improve organization and productivity features

## Local Storage

Most user content is stored locally on the user’s device.

Savion does not continuously monitor personal activity beyond user-initiated actions such as sharing or saving content into the app.

## AI Processing

Some saved content may be securely processed using Google AI services to generate insights and summaries.

Only the content required for generating insights is transmitted securely.

## Permissions

Savion currently requests notification permission in order to:

* Send reminder notifications
* Deliver daily insights
* Provide helpful nudges

Savion does not request access to:

* SMS messages
* Contacts
* Call logs

## Third-Party Services

Savion may use trusted third-party services including:

* Google AI APIs
* Firebase services (if enabled in future versions)

These services may process limited information necessary for app functionality.

## User Control

Users may clear app data or uninstall the application at any time to remove locally stored information.

## Security

Savion uses industry-standard secure connections when communicating with external AI services.

## Children’s Privacy

Savion is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
